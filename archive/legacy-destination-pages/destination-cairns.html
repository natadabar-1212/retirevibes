Practical Basics

Getting there & getting settled

✈️
Getting There

~17–19 Hours from US

Cairns Airport (CNS) has direct international flights from Japan, Singapore, and other Asian hubs. From the US, most routes connect through Sydney or Brisbane (~3-hour domestic hop). Seasonal direct flights from Asia reduce the journey significantly.

🛂
Visa & Residency

eVisitor Visa (Subclass 651)

Free and applied for online, the eVisitor allows US citizens to stay up to 3 months at a time within an 18-month period. Australia has no dedicated retirement visa — longer-term residency requires a PR pathway. An immigration attorney can map your best options.

🏥
Healthcare

Private + World-Class Public

Australia's Medicare public system is available to permanent residents. Without PR, private health insurance is essential. Cairns Base Hospital is the major regional facility; private options are available in the city and surrounding area.
