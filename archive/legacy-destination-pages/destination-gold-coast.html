Practical Basics

Getting there & getting settled

✈️
Getting There

~15–17 Hours from US

Direct international flights to Gold Coast Airport (OOL) available seasonally from select US cities. Brisbane Airport (BNE), with many more direct routes, is 1 hour away. LA and San Francisco have the most convenient connections.

🛂
Visa & Residency

eVisitor Visa (Subclass 651)

Free and applied for online, the eVisitor allows US citizens to stay up to 3 months at a time within an 18-month period. Australia has no dedicated retirement visa — longer-term residency requires a PR pathway. Consult an immigration attorney for options.

🏥
Healthcare

Private + World-Class Public

Australia's Medicare public system is available to permanent residents. Without PR, private health insurance is essential. International-grade private hospitals are abundant on the Gold Coast, and costs are well below US equivalents.
