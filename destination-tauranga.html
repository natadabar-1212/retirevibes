Practical Basics

Getting there & getting settled

✈️
Getting there
13–15 Hours from US + Connection

Fly into Auckland International from Los Angeles, San Francisco, or New York via Air New Zealand — Auckland is NZ's main gateway. From Auckland, Tauranga is a 45-minute domestic flight or a comfortable 2.5-hour drive through the Waikato. Air New Zealand and Jetstar run frequent Auckland–Tauranga services. Tauranga Airport sits 8km from the city centre.

📋
Visa & Residency
Visitor Visa / Temporary Retirement Visa

US citizens can stay up to 90 days without a visa. For longer-term living, the New Zealand Temporary Retirement Visa requires NZ$750,000 invested in New Zealand plus NZ$60,000 annual income — renewable every two years. The Investor 2 visa is another pathway for those with NZ$3M to invest. The visa system has nuances, so engage a registered immigration adviser before making plans.

🏥
Healthcare
ACC + Private Insurance Recommended

New Zealand's ACC system covers accident treatment for all visitors at no cost. Non-accident public hospital care requires residency status. Americans living in Tauranga should carry comprehensive private health insurance — Southern Cross Health Insurance is the major New Zealand provider. Tauranga Hospital is a full district hospital, and the region has a well-established network of specialist clinics given its large retiree population.
