Practical Basics

Getting there & getting settled

✈️
Getting there
14–17 Hours from US

Most routes from the US East Coast connect through London, Amsterdam, or Johannesburg. Direct routes from Atlanta (Delta) operate seasonally. Cape Town International Airport is well-connected to major African and European hubs.

🛂
Visa & Residency
Retirement Annuity Visa

South Africa's Retirement Annuity Visa requires proof of a pension or monthly income of at least ~$1,500/mo. Valid for 4 years and renewable. Straightforward process with a local immigration attorney's help.

🏥
Healthcare
Excellent Private Hospitals

Cape Town's private hospitals (Netcare, Mediclinic) are world-class and significantly less expensive than the US. Private medical aid or international insurance is essential — the public system is not designed for expats.
