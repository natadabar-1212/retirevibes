The beach is the bonus, not the headline

Powdery white sand on Siesta Key, sure. But the actual reason Sarasota stays interesting is the Ringling Museum, Asolo Rep theater, Van Wezel concerts, and a Main Street that didn't get suburbanized. Cultural infrastructure normally found in much bigger cities.

☀️

Outdoor life, year-round

Seventy degrees in February. Kayaking in Sarasota Bay, biking the Legacy Trail through pine flatwoods, a sunset cruise out of Marina Jack, beach walks before breakfast. The pace shifts seasonally — quieter in summer — but it never really stops.

📋

Easy mode for the practical stuff

No state income tax. Medicare works exactly as you've already mapped out. No language barrier, no visa, no FBAR filings. Every realtor, doctor, and accountant speaks the same language you do. Less to figure out, more to enjoy.

👨‍👩‍👧

Close to family without commuting back

Direct flights to most US cities and grandkids three hours away on the East Coast. Same time zone, same currency, same holidays. The "still here when needed" version of retirement, with the climate upgrade.

What a Tuesday in Sarasota feels like.

You wake up to bright morning light through the shutters and the smell of salt air through the open window. Coffee on the lanai, the lizard family that lives in the garden going about their morning. The sky is doing its Florida thing — that particular shade of blue that doesn't quite happen anywhere else.

Late morning, a slow ride down the Legacy Trail by bike — eighteen paved miles through pine flatwoods and old citrus groves, mostly flat. Or downtown to the Saturday farmer's market for the week's produce, then a long lunch at the cafe under the banyan tree. Afternoon nap, the way you've earned the right to take it.

Evening at Asolo Rep — they're doing Chekhov this season — or a sunset cruise out past the lighthouse. Dinner at one of the Main Street places that's been here since the seventies. By ten you're walking back across the Ringling Bridge and the bay is silver. You don't keep track of the time. This is your regular Tuesday now.

The practical stuff

Three things to know before you go further.

Healthcare

Medicare + Medigap

At 65 you age into Medicare. Original Medicare paired with a Medigap plan covers most hospital and outpatient costs. Sarasota Memorial Hospital is the major facility — nationally top-ranked, a teaching hospital, and one of the most respected systems on the Gulf coast. Plenty of cardiology, orthopedic, and primary care options.

Taxes

Florida-friendly, with caveats

Florida has no state income tax — IRA withdrawals, pensions, and Social Security stay federal-only. Property taxes run ~1.0–1.3% of assessed value, with a $50K homestead exemption for primary residents. Sales tax is 7%. Most retirees come out ahead, but insurance and property tax costs are real.

Risk

Hurricane country, honestly

Sarasota's Gulf coast position means storm surge and wind are real considerations. Direct landfalls are uncommon, but Tampa-area systems can swing through. Newer construction (post-2002) meets stricter wind codes; older homes need careful inspection. Flood insurance is separate from homeowners and required in many zones.
