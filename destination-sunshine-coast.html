Practical Basics

Getting there & getting settled

✈️
Getting there
15–20 Hours from US

Fly into Sunshine Coast Airport (MCY) via Brisbane or Sydney — most US routings connect through one of these hubs. Brisbane Airport is an hour south and offers more US routing options. Air New Zealand and Qantas offer US–Australia service from Los Angeles, San Francisco, and Dallas.

📋
Visa & Residency
eVisitor / Investor Visa

US citizens can visit Australia visa-free on the eVisitor (651) for up to 3 months, free to apply online. Australia discontinued its Investor Retirement Visa (405). For longer-term living, most Americans explore the Business Innovation and Investment Visa (188) or the Contributory Parent Visa (143) if they have adult children here. An immigration attorney is essential — Australia's visa system is complex.

🏥
Healthcare
Private Insurance Required

Australia's Medicare system covers permanent residents and citizens — not temporary visitors or visa holders. As an American, private health insurance is essential. Australia has excellent private hospitals across the Sunshine Coast, with many US-trained specialists. Comprehensive private cover typically runs A$200–400/month. Quality of care is high across both public and private systems.
