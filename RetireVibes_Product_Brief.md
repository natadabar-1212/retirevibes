> **Changelog v1.1 → v1.2 (2026-04-26)**
> - **Brand line clarified — RetireVibes never makes claims about the user's money.** We don't ask the user about income, savings, or current spending, so any "your money goes further" or "more affordable for you" copy is overreach. > - Example destination taglines reworded to drop "affordable" framing. Cost framing is still allowed when it describes the destination or compares destinations (e.g. "Mérida costs roughly half what Sarasota costs") — but never the user's relationship to that cost.
> - Audience research note (§3): the "2–3× further abroad" insight remains valid as research that informs which destinations we feature, but is no longer a user-facing copy hook.

> **Changelog v1.0 → v1.1 (2026-04-25)**
> - Vibe Quiz reduced from 10 to 8 questions — removed both age sliders (current age, target retirement age). RetireVibes does not ask demographic data it doesn't need to power vibe-based matching.
> - Q2 ("Where do you see yourself waking up every morning?") changed from single-choice to multi-select "pick up to 2" and added "By a lake or river" as a sixth option (separate from beach).
> - Q4 ("Where in the world are you open to retiring?") regions cleaned up: "US only" → "United States" (the multi-select naturally captures "only this" by being the sole pick); the awkward "The Americas (Mexico, Central/South America, Canada)" group split into separate "Canada" and "Mexico & Latin America" options. 7 options total, all logically distinct.
> - Q8 (values question) capped at "pick your top 3" to prevent decision fatigue at the end of the quiz, and "Financial security" cut from the options — financial security is captured by Q5 lifestyle level and pulls the question off-brand from a vibe-driven framing. 6 vibe-aligned options remaining.
> - Results Page no longer includes nest-egg math ("you'd need ~$800K–$1M saved"). Per-month cost of living stays, but all retirement-savings calculations are explicitly handed off to financial advisor affiliates. This resolves a contradiction in v1.0 between "not a financial planning tool" and the rough-numbers section.
> - Welcome screen copy updated to lead with "See where your money goes 2–3× further" — the affordability reframe is the product's emotional payoff and the strongest motivator for the 40–55 audience (per audience research).

# **1. Product Vision**

RetireVibes is a fun, vibe-driven web app that helps Americans aged 40–55 figure out where they'd love to retire and get a rough sense of what it will cost — then keeps them engaged as their journey evolves over time.

| *Retirement planning shouldn't feel scary. RetireVibes makes it feel exciting.* |
| --- |

The app is intentionally NOT a financial planning tool. It is the spark that gets people excited and thinking seriously about retirement — often for the first time. It hands users off gracefully to deeper resources when they're ready.

## **What It Is NOT**

- Not a financial planning tool or retirement calculator

- Not intimidating or form-heavy

- Not a one-time quiz with no reason to return

- Not another generic retirement website

# **2. Brand Identity**

| **Name** RetireVibes **Tagline** "Good RetireVibes Only" **Domain** retirevibes.com | **Tone** Warm, fun, aspirational — a knowledgeable friend, not a financial institution. **Feels Like** High-end travel magazine meets personalized Spotify Wrapped. **Visual Direction** Rich destination photography, warm palette, clean typography. |
| --- | --- |

# **3. Target Audience**

Primary users are US-born Americans between the ages of 40 and 55 who are:

- Starting to think seriously about retirement for the first time, or

- Have been thinking about it and want to explore their options, or

- Closer to retirement and need to evaluate the best paths forward

These users want to feel informed and hopeful — not judged or overwhelmed. They are desktop-comfortable, research-oriented, and will engage with a well-designed results page that has real substance.

| *Retirement locations are worldwide — US domestic and international destinations are both in scope. Discovering that the life they're imagining already exists — often somewhere they hadn't considered — is one of the app's most powerful moments.* |
| --- |

# **4. The Vibe Quiz**

The quiz is the heart of the product. It must feel fun and fast — like a personality quiz, not a tax form. 8 questions covering lifestyle preferences and vibe — no age, no money, no math.

## **Quiz Design Rules**

- One question per screen

- Progress bar visible throughout

- Beautiful destination visuals with each question

- Multiple choice for most questions

- "Select all that apply" or "pick up to 2" where multiple answers are valid

- No demographic data we don't need (no age, no income, no savings) — this protects the brand promise that RetireVibes is not a financial planning tool

| --- |

## **Account Creation (Optional)**

- Displayed at peak excitement — just before results are revealed

- Email + password input with clear value proposition

- Primary CTA: "Show me my RetireVibes"

- Skip option: subtle text link (not a button) — keeps it low pressure

- Value offered: Save results permanently, personalized destination guide, monthly inspiration content

- If skipped: persistent banner on results page offers account creation

# **6. Results Page**

The hero moment of the entire product. Should feel like opening a really good gift. Think high-end travel magazine meets personalized data reveal.

## **Reveal Mechanic — Reverse Countdown**

- Match #3 revealed first — "Not feeling it? See match #2" CTA

- Match #2 revealed — "Ready for your #1 match?" CTA builds suspense

- Match #1 — the hero reveal. Largest card, most dramatic animation, "Your #1 RetireVibes Match" badge. Most shareable moment.

## **Each Destination Card**

- Stunning hero image of the destination

- Destination name, country and flag emoji

- Vibe tagline — e.g. "Cobblestone streets, world-class wine, and a slower kind of European cool"

- Vibe match tags — e.g. Warm climate, Vibrant city, Budget friendly, Rich culture

- Lifestyle cost snapshot — "Your upscale lifestyle here: est. $3,200/month"

- Housing snapshot — "2BR apartment: ~$280K to buy / ~$1,100/month to rent"

- "Learn More" button linking to full destination profile page

## **Destination Profile (Learn More Page)**

- The Vibe — 2–3 sentences painting daily life

- Climate overview

- Cost of living breakdown (rough estimates, clearly labeled as estimates)

- Housing: rent vs. buy ranges

- Healthcare quality and rough expat insurance cost

- Expat community overview

- The Catch — one honest consideration (builds enormous trust)

- Best for summary

- Curated affiliate resources to go deeper

## **Retirement Profile Summary Panel**

Personalized sidebar summarizing who they are as a retiree — generated from quiz answers. Highly shareable. Creates "this is literally me" moment.

## **Cost Snapshot — Per Destination, Per Month**

The results page shows estimated monthly cost-of-living per destination, scaled to the user's chosen lifestyle level (Q5). It does NOT do nest-egg math — calculating how much a user needs to save to retire is intentionally handed off to financial advisor partners.

| *Example: "Your upscale lifestyle in Porto is estimated at ~$3,200/month — about half what the same lifestyle costs in coastal California. These are rough estimates; a financial advisor can help you build the actual plan."* |
| --- |

This keeps RetireVibes firmly on the "spark" side of the line — vibes, destinations, and lifestyle costs in (per-month). Net worth, savings targets, and withdrawal strategies out (handed to advisors).

## **Handoff Section — "Ready to Take the Next Step?"**

- Talk to a retirement financial advisor (SmartAsset / Zoe Financial affiliate)

- Browse homes in [destination] (Zillow / international real estate affiliate)

- Plan a scouting trip to [destination] (travel booking affiliate)

## **Save & Share**

- Account creation CTA for users who skipped earlier

- Social share button with pre-written caption: "Just found out my top retirement destination is Porto, Portugal 🇵🇹 — took the RetireVibes quiz and I'm kind of obsessed. What's yours? retirevibes.com"

# **7. Retention Strategy**

RetireVibes is a living retirement headquarters — not a one-time quiz. These five features form a natural engagement loop that keeps users returning as their life evolves.

| **Feature** | **How It Works** |
| --- | --- |
| **Evolving Profile** | Quiz answers become their profile. Updates when life changes. Powers all personalization across the app. |
| **Destination Following** | Save and follow destinations. Receive updates as costs or conditions change. Becomes their Vibe Board. |
| **Monthly Inspiration Content** | Curated content served for their specific saved destinations. Feels like a magazine, not a newsletter. |
| **Quarterly Check-ins** | Email prompt: "A lot can change in 3 months — want to update your profile?" Keeps data fresh, users returning. |
| **Scouting Trip Nudge** | 7–10 day retirement scouting itinerary for their top destination. Takes retirement from abstract to real. Natural travel affiliate hook. |

# **9. User Accounts**

Lightweight accounts — email and password only. No lengthy profile setup. The quiz answers become the user's profile automatically.

## **Account Creation Flow**

- User completes quiz → reaches transition / processing screen

- Email + password prompt presented with clear value proposition

- Skip option available as subtle text link (not a button)

- If skipped: persistent banner on results page offers account creation

- Returning users: "My RetireVibes" in nav → login → saved results

## **What an Account Unlocks**

- Saved destination matches — permanent and accessible anytime

- Ability to update quiz answers as life changes

- Destination following and Vibe Board

- Monthly inspiration content for saved destinations

- Quarterly check-in prompts

- Scouting trip recommendations

# **10. Monetization Strategy**

RetireVibes' audience is commercially valuable. The strategy launches lean with affiliate revenue and scales into additional streams as the product grows.

## **Phase 1 — Launch (Day One)**

- **Affiliate partnerships:** Financial advisor matching (SmartAsset, Zoe Financial), real estate platforms, travel booking for scouting trips, expat health insurance, international moving services.

- **Email list building:** Highly targeted list of retirement-minded 40–55 year old Americans. Opens doors to sponsored placements and future partnerships.

## **Phase 2 — With Audience**

- **Sponsored destination spotlights:** Tourism boards and regional authorities pay for featured placement. Portugal, Panama, and Mexico all have active retirement attraction programs.

- **Freemium / RetireVibes Pro:** Free tier stays generous. Pro unlocks additional destination matches, full Vibe Board, priority resources. Priced ~$7.99/month or $59/year.

## **Phase 3 — At Scale**

- **Retirement relocation concierge:** Premium human-assisted service for users ready to make the move. High ticket, low volume, high margin.

- **Brand partnerships:** Co-branded content and features with companies marketing to retirees in healthcare, leisure, and financial services.

| *Launch Note: Free tier only for MVP. Freemium Pro is planned but not built at launch. The product is architected to support it. When users start asking for more destinations or advanced features, that's the signal to build and charge for Pro.* |
